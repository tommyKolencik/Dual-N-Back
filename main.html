The practice / A quick guide

How to play.

What does “N” mean?

N is how many cues back you compare. At 2-back, compare cue 3 with cue 1, cue 4 with cue 2, and so on.

  1. Watch the square. Press A when its position matches N cues ago.
  2. Listen to the letter. Press L when the letter matches N cues ago.
  3. Decide separately. Press both if both match, or neither if neither matches.

The first N cues build your memory; no responses are needed. You can mark a match until the next cue arrives.

Feedback as you go

Right means you caught a repeat. Wrong means you pressed when it didn't repeat. After each cue, you'll also see any missed matches and correct passes (no press needed).

Start with 1-back if you're learning. Pause takes a break; Reset abandons the attempt without saving a score.
